# Huroob Check Online — Free Iqama & Border Number Status Check
[Huroob Check Online](https://www.ashtartradetest.com/tools/huroob-check) is a free tool from Ashtar Trade Test & Technical Training Centre, Multan, Pakistan. It lets workers check their iqama or border number status — including huroob status — before they travel or apply for Gulf jobs.
## Why check before you travel
Many Pakistani workers head to Saudi Arabia and the wider Gulf for employment. A huroob (absconding) record on an iqama can block a visa, a transfer or a return to work. Checking the status first, with the iqama or border number, takes a few minutes and can save a wasted trip.

## Trade-test preparation
A trade test is a hands-on exam set by Gulf employers or manpower agencies: the worker performs a real task and the result decides the job offer. Ashtar prepares beginners up to test standard step by step, and helps experienced workers sharpen a specific skill, practise the test task, and walk in ready. Ashtar offers Job Placement Assistance for students who complete their training and trade-test preparation.
